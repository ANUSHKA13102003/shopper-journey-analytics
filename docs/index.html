1. Session Funnel Drop-Off (REES46, 109.95M Events)

What this means: 89.96% of browsing sessions leave before adding an item to cart (10.04% view-to-cart), and 59.41% of carts abandon before payment. Median time from first view to purchase is 3.12 minutes.

2. New-Shopper Cohort Retention (REES46, 4.36M Eligible Users)

What this means: Excluding the first 7 days as existing traffic, cumulative return reaches 27.51% within 7 days, 37.96% within 14 days, and 49.74% within 28 days, while 32.24% of first-time buyers repeat within 28 days.

3. Randomized Ad Uplift Experiment (Criteo v2.1, 13.98M Users)

What this means: Across an 85/15 split (max |SMD| = 0.0488), treatment lifts overall conversion by +1.15 per 1,000 users (+59.4% relative, p = 7.31e-179), but top-quartile segments (F10 > 5.3 / F4 > 10.28) generate +15.51 per 1,000 (13.5x average).

Decision: Target High-Uplift Segments First — Do Not Scale Blanket Ad Spend

At an assumed conversion value of 1,000, blanket ad targeting breaks even only below 1.15 per treated user, whereas the F10/F4 top quartile breaks even up to 15.51 per user. Fix product-page add-to-cart friction (89.96% drop-off) and checkout abandonment (59.41%) first, then fund targeted ad segments where expected lift clears ad cost.
